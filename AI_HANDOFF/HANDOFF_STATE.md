Decision D-51 (option B): Action is an independent business entity and a MLINO recommendation is one of four origins - recommendation, decision, automated, undeclared. The owner's question "should provenance belong to the Action or stay outside" was found to ask two things at once: record provenance (how do we know the action happened) and origin (why, and on whose authority). Both live on the action, kept separate, which is the ADR-0006 lesson applied again; and of the four sources the owner listed, "external system" is a different kind - it is record provenance (INTEGRATION), not an origin, because an external system has no authority to decide for the business. authorizedBy is always a human or explicitly unknown and MLINO is never the authoriser (D-43); it is mandatory and stored on the action for recommendation origin, because nothing else holds the accepting human, and is read from the Decision rather than duplicated for decision origin. undeclared requires a reason, and two forms of fabrication are both forbidden - backfilling a Decision, and wrapping a human choice as a Recommendation. AI_INFERRED cannot record an action, but a model may raise a Signal that an unrecorded action seems to have occurred, which a human then records - a way to surface forgotten actions without fabricating them. Three findings go beyond the framing.
