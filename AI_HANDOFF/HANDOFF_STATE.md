The important part of this pass is that TWO THINGS I HAD DESIGNED WRONG were corrected rather than quietly reworded. First, version 0.2 modelled Evidence as a STAGE in the chain between Observation and Signal; it is in fact a supporting dimension attached to knowledge claims, giving the final structure Knowledge Item plus Evidence plus Provenance plus Confirmation, and Evidence is explicitly NOT a replacement for Observation. Second, version 0.2 carried a rule I had invented myself - "a Signal never references an Observation directly" - justified by the claim that two parallel paths would break traceability. That justification started from a false premise: Evidence was never meant to be the route to an Observation, it is support, not an intermediary, so no parallel path exists to break. The rule was removed and a Signal now derives from observations and/or evidence, with at least one of the two required. A row in the decisions register, D-40, also carried the wrong chain wording from that same mistake; it was corrected in place and marked as having been wrong rather than silently rewritten. PHASE_2_TYPES went back from four members to three because Evidence is not a knowledge type. The owner's own reasoning for barring AI_INFERRED from creating Evidence is sharper than the one I had written and was adopted into the document: an AI can produce interpretations, but cannot become its own proof source, because a model that generates both the claim and its proof closes a loop that cannot be falsified from outside. One new rule follows from D-44 and was reasoned rather than assumed: evidence on an Observation is OPTIONAL, because an observation stands on its provenance, and forcing evidence would mean recording the same booking-system record twice, once as origin and once as proof, which adds no information.
