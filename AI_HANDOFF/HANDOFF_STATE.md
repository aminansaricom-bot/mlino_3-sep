Decision D-50: EvaluationRecord holds two independent judgments - effectiveness (did the measured quantity improve against its reference) and attribution (did our action cause it) - each with its own level, basis and origin; effective: boolean is removed rather than deprecated; attribution never exists outside an evaluation; temporal association alone never exceeds "possible"; Action, Outcome and Evaluation each get their own identity; Outcome gains a baseline and an observed value and rejects AI_INFERRED under the same rule as Observation (D-39); and a causal claim can enter standing knowledge only with attribution of at least "probable" plus explicit human confirmation. Four points go beyond the question as posed. First, "option A" was split into a naive and a proposed form and the naive form rejected: adding identity and provenance to the same boolean fixes problems one and two but leaves problem three, and making an ambiguous claim auditable does not make it clear. Second, the boolean errs in both directions, not only the familiar one: effective: false is read as "the action failed" even when a competitor offset a working action, and "this does not work" is itself a causal claim needing the same bar. Third, a fourth problem was found in OutcomeRecord itself: it holds only free text with no baseline, so an outcome is a number rather than a change and even effectiveness has no structured ground. Fourth, the decisive reason against a separate Attribution entity is that it permits a causal claim with no evaluation behind it; forcing an evaluation parent reduces it to the chosen design with separate storage, which is a persistence decision deferred to that phase's CCR, so under D-50 the owner's first principle becomes structural rather than procedural. Verified rather than assumed: no service, adapter or repository consumes ActionRecord, OutcomeRecord or EvaluationRecord - the only references are build output and a test comment - so removing effective now is nearly free. A stated limit: validation checks shape, not honesty - it can require alternativesConsidered to be non-empty for "probable" but cannot verify the alternatives were really considered, which is why the final gate into standing knowledge stays human. No ADR was written: this applies the ADR-0005 principle one level deeper and leaves ADR-0005's structure intact, the same judgement made for D-45 relative to ADR-0006.
