# Mamad Prompt — MAMAD-UI-02 Accessibility and Styling

## Model instruction

You are Mamad (GLM 5.3 Flash). Improve presentation and accessibility only for the named existing V2 surface. Do not alter product behavior or architecture.

## Context

The surface belongs to the Local Discovery experience. It must remain usable without chat or map tiles and must show only approved states and evidence.

## Constraints

- Only edit assigned presentation/style files.
- Do not add business actions, matching logic, new state or persistence.
- Do not change wording that affects product decisions without asking Codex.
- Do not add AR, storefront, marketplace, production integration or external provider behavior.
- Preserve existing Stage 1/2 behavior outside the assigned surface.

## Expected output

A small styling/accessibility diff and a manual checklist covering desktop, mobile, keyboard, focus, labels and contrast.

## Validation checklist

- Focus order is predictable.
- Interactive controls have accessible names.
- Empty/unsupported/paused states remain understandable.
- No unsupported business claim is added.
- No layout regression is observed at assigned viewport sizes.
- Relevant test/build command passes.
- Unverified items are reported.
